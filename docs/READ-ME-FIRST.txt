CA DESK {{VERSION}} - WINDOWS (64-BIT)

1. Extract ALL files from CA-Desk-v{{VERSION}}-win-x64.zip into a folder.
2. Open that folder and double-click CA-Desk.exe.
3. Click Add Client, then open its workspace and add work areas.

No installation, administrator access, .NET download, account or internet
connection is needed to use the application. Keep all extracted files together.
The application begins with an empty workspace.

Changes are automatically saved in:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\CA Desk\ca-desk.db

To update: close CA Desk, extract the newer release into a new folder and
launch its CA-Desk.exe. Your existing database remains in your Windows profile.

Dashboard: click a summary card to view matching work.
Clients: switch between cards and compact table, or open a client workspace.
Master Work Table: combine search and filters; edit status and dates directly.
Calendar: choose Month, Week or Day; click an entry to edit its details.
Settings: see the local database location and application version.

Keyboard: Ctrl+F focuses page search; Ctrl+N adds a client; Ctrl+R refreshes.
Enter saves an open form; Escape cancels. The top-right title-bar buttons
minimize, maximize/restore and close CA Desk. Double-click its title bar to
maximize/restore, or drag the title bar to move the window.

Exactly three statuses: Not Started, In Progress and Completed.
Red deadline dates indicate overdue unfinished work. Completing the work removes
it from the overdue count. Removing a deadline is supported.

Distribution is portable and unsigned. No installer is included.
Test evidence and remaining platform limits: see the GitHub release notes.
Private repository: sign in to your GitHub account to download releases.
https://github.com/Fahad-uz/CA-Desk/releases
